# Discussion Hub Stewardship and Governance

This document describes how the public Root Sequence discussion hub is maintained. It is not a governance charter for every project in the ecosystem; each project retains responsibility for its own decisions, access boundaries, and canonical records.

## Principles

- **Clarity** — identify what is being discussed and where durable work belongs.
- **Transparency** — leave useful context, links, and decision records where they can be public.
- **Plurality** — preserve uncertainty and substantive disagreement.
- **Accountability** — name who can act, what was decided, and what remains open.
- **Proportionate process** — do not turn a lightweight conversation into authority it does not have.
- **Care and privacy** — public discussion never overrides consent, confidentiality, or project-specific safety boundaries.

## Roles

### Maintainers

Maintain the discussion space, clarify scope, moderate according to the Code of Conduct, and route concrete work toward the repository or project that owns it.

### Participants

Ask questions, share relevant context, test ideas, identify tensions, and help summarize what a conversation established or left unresolved.

### Project stewards

People authorized within a particular project may explain its boundaries and carry an agreed action into that project. Participation in a discussion does not by itself grant project authority.

## How decisions travel

1. A discussion explores a question or proposal.
2. Participants distinguish evidence, interpretation, preferences, risks, and unresolved disagreement.
3. The thread identifies the project or document that has authority to maintain the outcome.
4. Concrete work proceeds through that project’s normal process, such as an issue, pull request, approval record, or steward decision.
5. The discussion links to the durable result and notes what remains open.

Polls can reveal preferences but do not automatically establish consensus, consent, technical validity, or authority. A discussion can inform a decision without becoming the canonical decision record.

## Evolution

This model is intentionally lightweight. Update it when the hub’s real stewardship practices change, not merely to make the process sound more complete than it is.
